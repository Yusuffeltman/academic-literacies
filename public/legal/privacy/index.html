Academic Literacies

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Academic Literacies application processes, why that information is processed, who can access it, and what rights students and staff have in relation to that information.

Status

This page is based on the app's current governance and DPIA documents and is intended for operational transparency and Google Cloud / OAuth configuration. The underlying governance material is marked draft pending institutional legal review.

Last updated: 2026-04-06

Who We Are

Academic Literacies is a course application used for ALE00Y1, "Academic Literacies in the Age of AI", at the University of Johannesburg. The application supports student learning, reflection, communication, and course administration.

The responsible party for personal information processed through the course is the institution, acting through the Academic Literacies teaching team and relevant institutional data-governance structures.

Information We Process

Depending on how the application is used, the app may process:

  • account and profile details such as name, email address, user role, and institutional identifiers
  • course participation data such as module progress, session activity, navigation events, submissions, quiz attempts, and discussion activity
  • student-authored content such as writing submissions, reflections, questionnaire responses, and learning-plan records
  • support and communication data such as announcements, scheduled sessions, tutor notes, and related course interactions
  • limited technical and security information needed to operate the app, troubleshoot issues, and protect the service

Why We Process It

The application processes information to:

  • deliver course content and core learning activities
  • help students see their own progress and development over time
  • help assigned tutors support students more effectively
  • help the teaching team understand cohort-level patterns and improve the course
  • maintain the security, availability, and integrity of the platform

According to the current governance documents, the app's learning-analytics processing relies primarily on legitimate interest and contractual necessity within the course context, rather than consent alone.

Who Can Access Information

Access is role-based and limited to what is necessary:

  • students can access their own information only
  • assigned tutors can access information for students assigned to them
  • the teaching team can access aggregated cohort-level views, not individual student records for analytics purposes
  • system administrators may have technical access for maintenance, security, backup, and support purposes

Teaching-team cohort views are designed to suppress small groups and prevent individual identification.

Retention

The current data-governance specification states that individual-level analytics data is retained until the end of the academic year in which it was collected, after which it is deleted according to the retention schedule. Aggregated statistics that no longer identify individuals may be kept for course-improvement purposes.

Security

The application applies technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect personal information, including authenticated access, role-based visibility controls, audit logging, and controlled data-management processes. No system can guarantee absolute security, but the app is designed to limit unnecessary exposure and restrict access by role.

Your Rights

Based on the current governance documents, students may request:

  • access to personal information held about them
  • correction of certain self-provided information
  • objection to certain processing on reasonable grounds, subject to legal and academic review
  • deletion of individual-level data after the course ends
  • information about how to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator

The current governance documents reference the South African Information Regulator at https://www.justice.gov.za/inforeg/.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party infrastructure and APIs needed to operate the service, including hosting, authentication, analytics, email, calendar, and AI features where enabled. Those services process requests as part of delivering application functionality.

Where Google or Microsoft integrations are enabled by the user, the application may access the connected account only for the functionality requested by the user, such as reading calendar events or sending email through connected services.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or app-specific handling of data, contact the Academic Literacies module team through official course channels. For formal privacy-rights requests, students should use the University of Johannesburg's official Information Officer / POPIA contact route.
