## Scale (1-4)
- 1 = Emerging (mostly dependent)
- 2 = Developing (partial agency with prompting)
- 3 = Proficient (mostly self-directed)
- 4 = Strong (independent and transferable)

## Criteria

### 1) Inquiry Ownership
- 1: Needs full direction to define a question.
- 2: Can define a question with heavy tutor prompting.
- 3: Defines a focused inquiry question with minor prompting.
- 4: Defines and refines a focused inquiry question independently.

### 2) Choice Quality (bounded autonomy)
- 1: Chooses options randomly or without rationale.
- 2: Makes a choice but rationale is weak.
- 3: Makes an appropriate choice with clear rationale.
- 4: Makes a strategic choice aligned to goals and constraints.

### 3) Learning Strategy Control
- 1: Relies on one fixed approach regardless of results.
- 2: Attempts strategy changes only when directed.
- 3: Adjusts strategy based on feedback/evidence.
- 4: Proactively tests and optimizes strategies.

### 4) Evidence Judgement and Verification
- 1: Accepts information at face value.
- 2: Verifies inconsistently.
- 3: Applies source checks and justifies evidence selection.
- 4: Critically triangulates and explains confidence limits.

### 5) Reflection Depth (double-loop)
- 1: Describes task completion only.
- 2: Identifies what was learned but not how learning changed.
- 3: Explains what changed and why strategy shifted.
- 4: Connects strategy shifts to future transfer contexts.

### 6) Transfer Readiness
- 1: Cannot apply learning beyond current task.
- 2: Applies with extensive scaffolding.
- 3: Applies to a new context with light support.
- 4: Independently transfers and adapts learning to new contexts.

## Interpretation Guide
- 6-11: Agency foundations need explicit support.
- 12-17: Agency developing; maintain bounded choice and coaching.
- 18-21: Agency proficient; increase independent challenge.
- 22-24: Agency strong; add extension/leadership tasks.

## ILP Link Actions
- If total <= 11: add scaffolded planning + mandatory strategy conference.
- If total 12-17: retain pathway, add one targeted agency goal.
- If total >= 18: add opt-up or independent inquiry extension.
