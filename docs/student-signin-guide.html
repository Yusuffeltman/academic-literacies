Open your email inbox

Switch to your email app (Gmail, Outlook, etc). Look for an email from noreply@academic-literacy.firebaseapp.com. It may take a minute to arrive.

5

Tap the sign-in link in the email

Tap "Sign in to Academic Literacies" in the email. This will open the app and sign you in automatically.

6

You're in!

Your student dashboard will load with your course content, activities, and progress. You do not need a password — just use the email link each time you sign in.

Tips & Troubleshooting

  • You must use the same email address that your lecturer has on the class list
  • The sign-in link expires after a short time — if it doesn't work, go back and request a new one
  • Check your Spam / Junk folder if you don't see the email within 2 minutes
  • You do not need a password — the email link is your password
  • If you open the link on a different device, you may need to type your email again
  • If you still can't sign in, contact your lecturer with your student number
