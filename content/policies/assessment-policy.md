## Assessment Policy — Differentiated Rewards

This document declares the `governance/differentiated-rewards-framework.jsx` file as the formal assessment policy for the ALE00Y1 programme.

Purpose
- Ensure assessment recognition is equitable across tiers by rewarding process, growth, persistence, metacognition, and quality of work rather than relative rank.

Scope
- Applies to all formative and summative assessments in ALE00Y1, including quizzes, writing tasks, portfolios, and tutorial activities.

Key reference
- Governance file: governance/differentiated-rewards-framework.jsx

Implementation note
- Teachers and assessment designers should consult the reward dimensions and tier-parallel rubrics in the governance file when writing rubrics, marking guides, and student feedback templates.

For full guidance, open the governance resource in the repository: `governance/differentiated-rewards-framework.jsx`.
